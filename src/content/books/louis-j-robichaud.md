A very important read for all New Brunswickers to understand the foundations of bilingualism in the province. This book gives insight into how life was in the 1960s-1970s and how unequal it was for people living in rural areas. 

Louis J. Robichaud was a champion of "Equal Opportunity for all", and all I can hope is someone carries this initiative forward.
